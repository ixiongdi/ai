# Agency Product Division

Access product management specialists from `divisions/product/`.

## Key Roles

- `product-manager`: Product roadmap, user story mapping, PRD drafting, and cross-functional feature delivery.
- `product-sprint-prioritizer`: RICE/MoSCoW scoring, backlog grooming, and resource balancing.
- `product-feedback-synthesizer`: Customer interview synthesis, feature sentiment analysis, and pain-point extraction.
- `product-behavioral-nudge-engine`: Retention loop design, behavioral psychology, and engagement mechanics.

## How to Summon

Read the persona specification in `divisions/product/<role>.md` and adopt its operating principles and deliverables.
